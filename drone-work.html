The other half

Every one of these was flown on something I built.

There's no off-the-shelf aircraft behind this footage. Frames chosen for the job, motors and ESCs matched to the payload, everything soldered, flashed and tuned on the bench, then repaired at the field when a gate came up faster than expected.

That bench work is what carried straight over into everything else here.
