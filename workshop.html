The workshop

Where everything else gets built.

A bench built from 2x4s and plywood, fitted out with power, light, a soldering station, a print station and a wall of tools. It's 99% done, and it changes every time a new kind of project shows up.

Overview

Built for the work, not bought for it.

Every print, repair and rack build on this site starts here. I framed it and topped it myself, then kept building onto it until each kind of work had its own place to happen.

What's built in

Four stations, one bench.

01

Power and lighting, built in

Outlets where the tools plug in and light over the work surface, so nothing runs off an extension cord across the floor and nothing gets worked on in its own shadow.
