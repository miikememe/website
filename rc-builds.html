On the bench

Assembled, wired, and tuned.

RC cars, quads, and whatever else has a battery and a problem worth solving.

Same skills, different chassis.

An RC car is a drone with the lift taken out: a brushless motor, an ESC, a receiver, a battery and a pile of decisions about gearing and grip. Everything I learned keeping quads in the air transfers straight across — and it's a lot cheaper to crash.

Builds from a kit or from a box of parts, upgrades, wiring that won't shake loose, and the diagnostic work when something runs for thirty seconds and then doesn't. If it's electronic and it moves, I'm interested.
