The through-line

It all runs through the same bench.

FPV sits at the intersection of everything I like: electronics, fabrication, and making a thing do something it shouldn't be able to. You don't fly a quad you didn't build — you solder it, you tune it, and you repair it at the field with the parts in your bag.

Those skills don't stay in one hobby. It's the same soldering iron on an RC car, the same CAD and slicer on a printed bracket, the same troubleshooting on a rack of servers in the basement. Different projects, one bench.
