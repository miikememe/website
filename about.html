A short intro.

I'm Mike — based in North Georgia. FPV drones pulled me in hard, and it's been a full-on rabbit hole ever since.

These days I split my time between flying, 3D printing parts for my rigs (and whatever else seems interesting), and tinkering with my homelab. I'm a general tech obsessive — if it has firmware or a slicer profile, I'm probably into it. When I'm not doing any of that, you'll find me at a concert. Big live music person, especially EDM.
